A key component was a strategic tax plan involving a fiscal year election. By structuring a significant portion of the company’s earnings as accrued compensation to stockholders, we reduced the immediate tax liability. This compensation was deferred to the following year, providing an ongoing one-year tax deferral. This approach ensured the company retained critical liquidity to fund operations and growth initiatives.'
